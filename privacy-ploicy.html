Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.kalkunnelchurch.directory

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how St. George Youth Association ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the കൽക്കുന്നേൽ പള്ളി mobile application and its companion web service (collectively, the "App").

1. Purpose & Restricted Access

The App is a private parish member directory intended exclusively for verified members of Kalkunnel Mar Geevarghese Sahada Church. Directory data is not publicly accessible. Every user account must be manually verified and approved by an administrator before any parish directory information or announcements can be accessed.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Registration Information: When you register for an account or request password assistance, we collect your email address, password, name, house/family name, place, contact phone number, parish membership confirmation, and any optional identification notes you provide to help administrators verify your identity.
  • Parish Directory Member Information: For approved users, the App displays parish directory records maintained by the parish, which may include full name, family/house name, place, church unit/ward, family registration number, family relationships, dates of birth/death, marriage dates, member photos, occupation/about details, postal addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and optional residential map coordinates.
  • User-Submitted Proposals & Messages: Approved users may optionally submit edit proposals, new-member proposals, member photos, or messages to parish administrators. These submissions are reviewed by administrators before any changes are made to the directory.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you enable notifications, we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration token associated with your approved account solely to deliver parish announcements.
  • Optional Diagnostic Logs: The App maintains a small local technical log in private app storage. This log is only shared or uploaded to administrators if you explicitly choose the "Share log" or "Send log to admin" action. Diagnostic logs contain app version, Android version, device model, and sync status metadata, and never include passwords, authentication tokens, or directory member records.

3. Device Permissions We Request

  • Internet & Network State: Used to authenticate your account, sync directory updates, and fetch parish announcements.
  • Post Notifications (Optional): Used to display parish announcements in your device notification tray.
  • Photos / Media Selection (Optional): Used only when you explicitly choose a photo from your device to propose a member profile picture.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for parish administration and community directory purposes:

  • To verify your identity as a parishioner and manage your account access.
  • To provide approved members with online and offline access to the parish member directory.
  • To deliver official parish announcements and notifications.
  • To review and apply member-submitted directory corrections and additions.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues when you voluntarily submit a diagnostic log.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use your personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage, Offline Sync & Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Private Cloud Storage: Backend data, member photos, and directory snapshots are stored in private, access-controlled cloud storage (powered by Supabase) protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) and short-lived signed URLs.
  • Local Offline Storage: To enable offline directory browsing, the App downloads a read-only SQLite snapshot into your device's private internal app storage, inaccessible to other apps. Authentication tokens are stored using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • Access Revocation: If an account is logged out or its approval is revoked by an administrator, local offline directory snapshots and cached account data are removed upon the next server check.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following trusted infrastructure providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Supabase: Provides database hosting, user authentication, and private file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers push notification wake-up signals for parish announcements (without exposing member personal data in push payloads).

7. Data Retention, Correction & Account Deletion

  • Directory Corrections: Approved users can propose updates or corrections to any member record directly inside the App ("തിരുത്തൽ നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക" / Propose Edit) or by messaging the administrator.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request deletion of your user account or removal/modification of your personal directory details at any time by contacting the parish office using the details below or via the in-app "അഡ്മിന് സന്ദേശം" (Message Admin) feature. Account deletion requests will be processed within 14 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a community directory intended for use by parish members and families. User accounts are restricted to administrator-approved individuals. Family directory entries for minors are published and managed with the consent and oversight of their family head and parish administration.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
